For this assignment, I designed bookmarks for a local elementary school. This was my first time working with Adobe InDesign as well as lithograph printing. Lithography is a type of printing process that involves first printing the design on an aluminum plate. Then, the plate undergoes a chemical process that turns the design into a relief that can hold ink for printing. Once dry, the plate is inserted to an offset lithography machine that evenly applies ink to the surface through a series of rollers. Once there is a visible, even, and rich color on the rollers, the plate is ready for a trial run! After a few test prints to ensure color quality and alignment, the machine is ready to mass print the image onto paper. Once printed, the pages were run through a large industrial paper cutter that could cut through large stacks all at once. This process is commonly used for mass printing such as bookmarks. All of the bookmarks were then taken to the elementary school's library where students could pick them up during the bookstore sale.
