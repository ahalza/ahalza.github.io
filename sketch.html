For this assignment, we learned how to draw various types of perspective, including Oblique, Isometric, Cavalier, Cabinet, and one and two-point perspective. Cavalier and Cabinet are two types of Oblique drawings that have different depths; with Cabinet being the more realistic view to what we'd normally see. These are common ways to create preliminary sketches of product designs. We also practiced drawing letters and boxes using each method, and creating isometric or oblique drawings from a top-view perspective. The final assignment was to design a small cereal box using both 2-point perspective and a flattened view of each side. We examined existing cereal boxes from Kellogg's and other brands as a reference for what to include, such as the barcode, cereal bowl and mascots, and nutrition information. I chose Pickle Puffs, a type of cereal that is pickle flavored. I also used the complementary colors red-pink and lime-green to develop a more interesting composition.
