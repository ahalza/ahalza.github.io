For this assignment, we learned both photo taking skills and photo editing techniques. First, I took a series of photos using a variety of methods, such as different types of lighting, the rule of thirds, asymmetry and symmetry, and perspective. Then, I chose the 4 best that I wanted to edit in Adobe Photoshop. This was my very first time using Photoshop, and I experimented with several different tools that I learned for the first time, such as the brush tool and center vignette effects. Below are each photos both before and after editing. Next to each are descriptions on how the photo was taken, what the subject matter is, as well as composition techniques used or applied through cropping and editing. The after photos also include specific details on the exact edits I made, such as color saturation values and resolution changes. My favorite is the picture of my cat, Teddy Bear!
